What transfers from oil and gas to wind.

Wind turbines and gas turbines produce the same class of operational data: tag-level telemetry at fixed intervals, alarm streams requiring rationalisation, and vibration signatures that indicate degradation ahead of any drop in output.

My background is that work in oil and gas — SCADA historians, DCS logic, alarm management, and rotating equipment condition monitoring. Currently contracted to TÜV Rheinland in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province.

What carries over directly: data quality assessment on production historians, alarm rationalisation, condition-based maintenance planning, and reconciling reported availability against source records.

Wind-specific work is deliberate and current. GWO certified. IEC 61400 methods implemented in code rather than referenced in slides. Tooling built and tested against real turbine datasets.
